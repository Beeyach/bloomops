NON-NEGOTIABLE SAFETY RULES

1. Reuse existing authorization and visibility authority.
Do not create a second definition of client-visible Content or Files.

2. Server-side authorization is mandatory.
Navigation hiding and browser filtering do not count as authorization.

3. Use exact allowlisted Client DTOs.
Do not serialize internal Content/File/domain rows and strip them client-side.

4. Preserve live revocation.
Current membership, contact linkage, workspace, Content visibility, parent scope, service validity and other canonical authorization facts must be rechecked according to existing C1-C5 semantics.

5. Do not expose internal-only information.
This includes internal owner/assignment facts, pillar/classification, revision/CAS values, private stage context, staff notes, internal activity/audit data, service or membership internals, storage keys, upload internals, request keys, hidden counts or unrelated Client data.

6. C5 approval snapshots are special review packages.
General C6 Content visibility does not automatically expose script, hook, caption, CTA or every field that may exist inside an explicit C5 review snapshot. Reuse the C5 approval route for requested review content.

7. Reuse C4 Files/R2.
Do not create another upload/download/storage system. Do not broaden recording retry or File visibility rules.

8. No schema by default.
C6 is a projection/portal phase over existing C1-C5 canonical facts. Do not add a migration for labels, nav state, counts, action flags, duplicate visibility or result caches. If an unavoidable data-model blocker appears, document the blocker instead of inventing schema outside the contract.

9. Do not weaken existing tests, auth limits, concurrency rules, visibility rules or runtime safety to make C6 pass.

10. No production-side changes.
Do not touch production, DNS, real email/SMS, payments, production Cloudflare resources, staging business data or Leadsthatbloom.
