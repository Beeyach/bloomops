## N2A — Useful client overview

Opening `/clients/:id` should answer what this client has purchased, what is happening now, what is due next, and what needs a response. Put these operational sections before administrative facts and contacts. Keep the existing full-page route, tabs, client identity/status/health, prospect origin link, and working edit/activation flows. Use the official Bloomsi assets and existing primitives, readable labels and restrained icons. No dot-separated metadata, giant count cards or invented activity.

### Current implementation to reuse

- `app/(internal)/clients/[id]/page.jsx` already gates the client through `requireShell`, `loadInternalClientResource`, `evaluate` and `getClient`. Its Overview currently shows details, health and contacts. Services, projects and onboarding are separate tabs.
- `listClientServices` in `services.mjs` filters services to the actor's assignments; `listProjects` in `projects.mjs` uses `projectReadCondition` and returns a bounded page with `hasMore`.
- `listActions` in `actions.mjs` already supports trusted server composition, client filters, current record predicates, bounded results and dependency-aware overdue labels.
- `onboardingView` refreshes membership, selects the canonical activation/latest instance and filters each item through existing authority. Its required-item progress must not be replaced with a raw count.
- Milestone and deliverable dates already live in their canonical modules. Their existing per-project lists are not a reason to query every project separately for an overview.

### Read model and visible behavior

1. **Purchased services:** show actual engagements with their service/package name and own status. Link to the existing Services tab. Preserve separate client relationship, health and engagement statuses.
2. **Current work:** show a short list of active projects and open tasks the actor can read, with canonical destinations and clear state. Cap each visible list at five and provide the relevant full-list link. Use a truthful additional-results indicator; never present the count of a truncated page as a total.
3. **Next deadline:** find the earliest upcoming date among readable, nonterminal projects, milestones, tasks and deliverables for this client. Show the record type, label, date and direct destination. Respect client calendar dates/timezone and existing dependency/overdue semantics. Keep past-due work distinct; do not relabel an overdue item as an upcoming deadline. With no eligible date, say so plainly.
4. **Open requests:** show unresolved onboarding items relevant to the actor, distinguishing client action from internal review. Preserve submitted-versus-verified and required-versus-optional meaning. Link to the canonical Onboarding tab; do not complete, verify or waive anything from the summary. Explain a not-created or inaccessible onboarding view accurately.
5. Keep detailed client facts, contact management and internal history available through existing controls/sections. Do not introduce fake client-preview, comments or notification controls before their slices work.

Reuse existing query/read predicates and domain formatters. Select eligible records before sorting/limiting. Do not compute a global earliest deadline by scanning only the first visible project page. Bound results and avoid one query per project, action, service or request. A small server-only composition module is appropriate; a second database, client-side private-record cache, new stored summary table or background sync is not.

Every query and derived count must retain workspace, live membership, typed-parent, assignment and visibility checks. Being allowed to view a client does not grant unrestricted access to all its work. Service-only, project-only and action-only assignments must not broaden. Client accounts remain on the real portal and cannot open this internal overview. Preserve equal unavailable responses for missing and inaccessible clients. No new mutation endpoint or schema change is expected for N2A.

Retain replaced/removed objects under the existing ready-file retention approach, but make their former URLs unavailable immediately. Do not blindly delete objects after an uncertain storage/database result.
