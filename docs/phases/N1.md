## N1A — Retry failed sheet saves

The first bounded slice preserves reviewed values after a failed or partially successful sheet save. Keep failed fields and their original comparison values in the mounted review, identify each error beside its field, and offer an explicit retry. Confirm already-applied values after a lost response without writing again. Recheck current profile/revision on every retry and refuse to overwrite a newer field. Preserve undo receipts for confirmed successes across partial retries. Undo and save status must describe the action accurately.

Pending previews require an explicit discard before another edit or sheet query/view replaces them. Ordinary link navigation and browser reload warn about unsaved values; this slice does not claim durable recovery after accepting a leave prompt, closing a crashed browser or losing the component. No draft is written into browser storage and no automatic retry is introduced. Full recovery across navigation/session boundaries, including client-side history navigation, remains N1B.
