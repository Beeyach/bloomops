### P5B implementation contract

The single immutable conversion receipt links one prospect to a canonical client and purchased service engagement. The same D1 transaction creates any needed Draft client/unlinked primary contact and Planned service, saves agreed scope on the receipt, and records client/service/conversion/stop history. Existing client/contact/lifecycle and open engagement scope remain unchanged; closed clients require another selection. Missing onboarding templates do not block recording the sale. New client name/contact/website use canonical validation.

A review fingerprint detects stale selections and scope; it is not authorization. Writes recheck current Owner/Admin prospecting authority, profile revision, selected client/contact/service snapshots and delivery state in SQL. Request identity plus a normalized intent hash makes lost-response retries idempotent; concurrent distinct requests cannot create two clients/services for one prospect. Receipts cannot be changed or deleted.

The receipt permanently stops the prospect and captures its current address, selected client contact address and immutable delivery recipient (up to three unique normalized addresses). Existing delivery preparation/submission guards consult these stops; database triggers enforce the same boundary. An in-flight or uncertain delivery to the prospect or captured address must be resolved first, preventing a conversion from racing an already claimed send. No provider call is introduced or enabled. Email remains paused except this necessary local no-send protection integration.
