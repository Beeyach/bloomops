PREVIOUS AUDIT BLOCKER TO RE-VERIFY
The first independent audit failed because Content navigation was conditional but direct /portal/content access was still available for Clients with no meaningful discoverable Content. It also found an old-Published-only case where navigation could appear while all C6 list views were empty.

The correction claims to fix this by making navigation and /portal/content use the same discoverable-content eligibility, where eligibility is the union of:
- currently readable non-Published Content that belongs in the C6 current/upcoming experience
- Published Content inside the supported 30-day recent window

The correction also claims:
- ineligible Clients are redirected from /portal/content to /portal
- Clients with an eligible destination may still see a valid empty selected subview when another C6 view contains discoverable Content
- old-Published-only Content does not activate navigation or the index
- documentation and browser assertions were corrected

Do not assume those claims are true. Prove them.
