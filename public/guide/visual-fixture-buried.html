About the practice

We have worked with individuals and couples since 2009, from a small room above the bakery on Mill Street. Sessions run fifty minutes.

Most people arrive after months of putting it off, which is normal and is nothing to apologise for. The first session is mostly listening.

There is no fixed programme. Some people come for six weeks, some for two years, and some come back after a long gap because something changed.

Fees are on a sliding scale. Nobody has ever been turned away over money, and that is not going to start now.
